WHOOP data privacy notice

This notice explains how WHOOP data may be used if you choose to connect your WHOOP account to a personal fitness-planning assistant.

Connection statusThe WHOOP connection is not active yet. This notice describes the data handling planned for the personal integration if and when it is enabled. No WHOOP data is currently being collected through it.

Who is responsible?

The personal integration is operated by Yael Abunniaj in Malta. Contact: yael.king23@gmail.com.

What data may be accessed?

If you authorize the connection, the app is intended to request read-only access to the following WHOOP data:

RecoverySleepWorkoutsDaily strain

The connection may also request permission to renew access (offline) so you do not need to authorize every session. It does not request permission to change WHOOP records.

Why is it used?

Selected WHOOP metrics may be used to tailor your exercise schedule, recovery suggestions, and general fitness guidance to you. If you ask the assistant to use these metrics, information needed for that request will be processed in the assistant conversation. The assistant provider’s own privacy terms apply to that processing.

Storage and retention

Planned handling if the connection is enabled: WHOOP OAuth credentials will be kept in the macOS Keychain on your Mac. The integration will not keep a separate local history of WHOOP metrics. When you ask ChatGPT to use your WHOOP data for a plan adjustment, only the selected values needed for that request will be included in the conversation. OpenAI handles that conversation under its EU privacy policy and the data controls available in your account. You can manage chat history and model-improvement settings in ChatGPT.

Your choices

Connection is optional. You can choose not to authorize it. You can revoke the app’s access through your WHOOP account’s connected-app settings. To remove credentials kept on your Mac, disconnect the integration and delete its WHOOP entry from Keychain Access. To ask a question or request deletion of data held by the personal integration, contact yael.king23@gmail.com. Data already included in an assistant conversation is managed through that service and your account settings.

Updates

This notice should be updated if the app’s permissions, data flow, storage, or purpose changes. Last reviewed: 16 October 2026.
